# Product vision

Project Together helps people turn vacations they want into vacations they actually take. The working product name and mascot name, Tavi, are unapproved internal names; branding must remain configurable.

The first customer is a working professional coordinating a private vacation with friends or family across different schedules, PTO limits, and budgets. The MVP outcome is an agreed set of feasible travel dates, rather than a generated itinerary.

Deliver useful PTO advice before registration, allow invitations to work in a browser, and keep individual constraints private. Explain calculations and assumptions. Government holiday calendars suggest dates; only employer-confirmed holidays count as days off. Pending leave remains pending until approved.

The product launches on iOS with a shared browser PTO and invitation experience. Android, stranger matching, memories, rewards, payments, advertising, and booking integrations are later work. Optimize for group agreement and repeat planning, rather than daily app opens.
