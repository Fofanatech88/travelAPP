# Assumptions and decisions to validate

| Hypothesis / assumption                                                                   | Why it matters                                | Validation / decision                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Working adults struggle more with reaching date agreement than generating itineraries     | Determines the central product value          | Interview recent group organizers and reconstruct a real planning attempt                   |
| Guests will respond through a short browser flow                                          | Invitation completion drives group usefulness | Observe phone-based invitation tasks; measure time, privacy comprehension and abandonment   |
| A useful PTO result is possible within about 60 seconds                                   | Enables value before registration             | Timed usability sessions; do not treat this as measured today                               |
| US federal baseline is an adequate first implementation surface                           | Keeps holiday verification tractable          | Label coverage; confirm every employer-observed day; collect demand for other jurisdictions |
| Recurring weekly schedules serve the initial audience                                     | Avoids premature shift modeling               | Record schedule patterns and unsupported needs in interviews                                |
| Participants accept sharing feasibility summaries while keeping exact PTO/budgets private | Shapes authorization and UX                   | Test disclosure language and inspect whether users can predict visibility                   |
| Explainable burden comparisons improve trust                                              | Total PTO alone can disadvantage one person   | Present competing scenarios and ask participants to explain ranking/compromise              |
| Lean managed services support early test volume economically                              | Bootstrap constraint                          | Track actual usage/costs once provisioned; no resources purchased in discovery              |

No customer research, market validation, pricing experiment or trademark clearance has been completed. Public name, domain, mascot artwork and positioning remain founder decisions. Additional-country coverage, carryover/accrual and group size/search limits need explicit requirements before implementation.
