No customer interviews or usability sessions have been conducted or recorded. Founder requirements are product direction, not evidence of customer validation.

For each session record: date, consent, anonymous participant label and relevant context, planning problem, observed task behavior, short anonymized quote only with permission, severity, proposed response and linked backlog item.
